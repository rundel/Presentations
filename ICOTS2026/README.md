# Teaching Statistical Computing in the Age of AI

Whether we are ready or not, AI has arrived and usage among students has become nearly ubiquitous. The rate of change of these tools has been extreme: what was mostly a novelty a couple of years ago is now capable of solving complex real-world problems. Keeping up with this rapid evolution presents a genuine challenge for instructors, requiring ongoing reassessment of what we teach and how we teach it.

In this talk we discuss how we are adapting to these changes in terms of course content, course policies, and course structure across multiple statistical computing courses at the undergraduate and master's level at Duke University. We will share what has worked, what has not, and how our thinking continues to evolve alongside the tools themselves.
